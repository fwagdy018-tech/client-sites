FAQ

Good to know

Do you come to me?

Yes. Perales Mobile Detailing is a mobile service: "We come to you" is how the shop describes it. Call or text to confirm they can reach your location.

Which areas do you serve?

Houston and surrounding areas, including Cleveland, TX. Not sure about your neighborhood? Call or text and ask.

How much does it cost?

Prices depend on the vehicle and the service, so there are no fixed prices posted. Send a free quote request or call and you'll get a straight answer.

How do I book?

Call or text +1 832-875-7249, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message to the shop. Nothing is booked until the shop confirms with you.

Can you remove odors and stains?

Google reviewers report smoke smells, odors and upholstery stains that were removed. Results depend on the condition of each vehicle, so ask for a quote and describe the problem.

What do you need at my location?

Details like water or power access aren't published, so call or text before your appointment and the shop will tell you what they need.
