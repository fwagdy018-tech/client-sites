FAQ

Good to know

Where does The Reflect Lab work?

The Google listing says “Serving McAllen & surrounding areas,” reviewers book from Edinburg, and the business site says it serves the R.G.V. There is no public shop address because the work is done at your location. Call or text to confirm they can reach you.

Do you come to me?

Yes. It is a mobile service. Reviewers say the detail was done at their home and that they did not have to sit at a shop.

What services do you offer?

Interior deep cleans, steam cleaning, odor and stain removal, engine bay cleaning and exterior wash and protection for cars, SUVs and trucks.

How much does it cost?

No price list is published. Cost depends on the vehicle and its condition, so call, text or send the quote form and you get a straight answer before work starts. One reviewer calls the pricing structure “very fair.”

What are your hours?

Google lists Sunday 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday 9 AM to 5 PM and Tuesday to Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM. Appointments are required, so call or text to pick a day and time.

How do I book?

Call or text +1 956-332-3242, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message to the business. Nothing is booked until they confirm the day and place with you.

How can I pay?

The Google listing shows credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments are accepted. Confirm with the business when you book.
