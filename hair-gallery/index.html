A specialty

Color correction, specifically.

The salon’s window says it plainly: color correction specialists. Reviewers describe walking in with orange, patchy or uneven color and walking out happy, and say the stylists were patient through a long process.

Color correction takes time. Call or text with a photo of where you are now and ask what is possible.

Questions

Before you come in.

Do I need an appointment?

Google reviews describe both booked visits and last-minute fits. Call or text first and the salon can tell you what is open.

What are your hours?

Monday 2 – 7 PM, Tuesday to Friday 10 AM – 8 PM, Saturday 10 AM – 7 PM. Closed Sunday. Please confirm before a holiday.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published online. Call or text 361-883-5155 for a quote for the service you want.

Can I bring inspiration photos?

Yes. Several reviewers say they showed Carmen photos and left with the look they brought in.

Where is the salon?

3765 S Alameda St #1101, Corpus Christi, TX 78411, in Doddridge Plaza on the first floor.
