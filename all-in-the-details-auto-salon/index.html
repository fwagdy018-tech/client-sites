Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Call or text (808) 371-5688 and ask.

Do you really come to me?

Yes — that’s the whole idea. Our mobile unit carries its own water and power, so we can detail your vehicle at home, at work or wherever it’s parked. All we need from you is a little room to work.

Which areas do you serve?

Killeen, Harker Heights, Fort Cavazos, Copperas Cove, Belton, Temple and Georgetown, plus the surrounding Central Texas area. If you’re not sure we reach you, call or text (808) 371-5688 and we’ll confirm.

How much does a detail cost?

Every job is quoted for your vehicle, because the price depends on its size, its condition and the service you choose. Send us the year, make and model and what you’d like done, and we’ll get back to you with a free quote. Reviewers regularly describe the pricing as fair and affordable.

What are your hours?

Every day, 10 AM to 6 PM, as listed on Google. Call or text to book a time — we’ll confirm the date and price with you before we come out.

What is paint correction, and do I need it?

Paint correction is careful machine polishing that removes swirl marks, haze, water spots and light scratches from the clear coat, instead of just hiding them under wax. If your paint looks dull or shows spiderweb swirls in the sun, it will make a dramatic difference — and it’s the right step before a ceramic coating.

What can you ceramic coat?

Cars, trucks, SUVs, classics — and also boats and RVs. We offer multiple coating packages; tell us what you drive and how you use it and we’ll recommend one.

Can you get out stains, pet hair and odors?

Yes. Shampoo and steam cleaning lift stains from seats and carpets, pet hair is removed and odors are treated at the source. Our reviews mention exploded soda, spills in cooled seats, kids’ car seats and dog hair — all handled.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the vehicle and the service. One customer’s full detail and wash on a truck took about two hours; paint correction and coatings take longer. We’ll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do you work on fleets?

Yes — fleet washing is one of our core services, done on site with our mobile unit. Call to set up a schedule for your company vehicles.

Do you offer a military discount?

One of our Google reviews mentions a military discount. Ask about it when you call or text to book.

What if the weather turns?

We keep you in the loop. If rain or a storm gets in the way, we’ll contact you and reschedule — customers mention exactly that in their reviews.
