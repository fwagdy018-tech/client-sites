Our story

Professional-grade detailing, without the drop-off.

All in the Details Auto Salon specializes in paint correction and ceramic and protective coatings, and offers professional-grade detailing at an affordable cost. The goal, in the owner’s own words, is “the highest level of quality and customer satisfaction creating a new industry standard.”

The business is run by owner Aaron DeMont and his crew — the name customers mention again and again in their reviews. Instead of a shop you have to drive to, everything happens from a self-contained mobile unit with its own water and power, so the detail comes to your driveway, office or job site.

That set-up has taken the team from family SUVs and work trucks to Corvettes, classics, boats, a 41-foot camper and company fleets across Killeen, Harker Heights, Fort Cavazos, Copperas Cove, Belton, Temple and Georgetown.

What makes us different

Four things customers notice first.

Not our slogans — the themes that come up over and over in 164 Google reviews.

Communication

Quick replies, updates along the way and photos during the job. “I was kept in the loop the entire process!”

Punctual & flexible

On time, early when you’re okay with it, and willing to work around weekends, rain delays and last-minute requests.

Obsessive detail

Seats pulled, consoles removed, pedals scrubbed, compartments opened. The little places are the point.

Fully mobile

Water tank, generator and pro equipment on board. “They come to you fully equipped, so you don’t have to worry about setting up water or supplies.”

Mission

Protect the investment. Respect the owner.

A vehicle is one of the biggest things most people buy. The mission is simple: bring it back to its best, protect it so it stays that way, and make the whole thing easy for you — from the first text to the final walk-around.
