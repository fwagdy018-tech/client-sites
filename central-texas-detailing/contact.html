FAQ

Good to know

Do you come to me?

Yes. Central Texas Detailing describes itself on Facebook as luxury mobile detailing, "from the comfort of your home," and reviewers say the detailer arrives at their house. Call or text to confirm your location.

Where are you based?

Facebook lists Austin, TX. There is no public shop address, so the best way to check your area is to call or text +1 512-694-1116.

How much does it cost?

There is no posted price list. Prices depend on the vehicle and the work, and reviewers say pricing is reasonable and that they received custom packages and quotes. Send a free quote request or call.

How do I book?

Google lists appointments as required. Call or text +1 512-694-1116, email info@centraltexasdetailing.com, or use the quote form here. The form prepares a text message, and nothing is booked until Central Texas Detailing confirms with you.

What services do you offer?

Instagram lists detailing, corrections and ceramic coatings. Reviews describe interior deep cleans, exterior details, maintenance details, trucks, SUVs, sports cars and an RV. Ask about anything else.

Is the business insured?

The Facebook page states that Central Texas Detailing is insured and has 4+ years of experience. Ask the owner if you need paperwork.

How can I pay?

Google lists credit cards, debit cards and checks as accepted payments. Confirm when you book.
