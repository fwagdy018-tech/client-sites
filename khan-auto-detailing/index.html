How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the service, your vehicle's size and the condition of the paint. Call or send a quote request with your vehicle details and you'll get a price for your specific car, truck or SUV.

What does ceramic coating actually do?

A ceramic coating bonds to your clear coat and forms a hard, glossy, water-repelling layer. It helps protect against UV, road grime and contaminants, makes washing easier and keeps the shine longer than wax. Multi-year options are available — ask which one fits your car.

Should I get paint correction before a coating?

Usually, yes. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so swirl marks and light scratches are typically corrected and polished out first. Khan can look at your paint and recommend what it needs.

Do you work on trucks and SUVs?

Yes — trucks and SUVs are welcome. Several of our Google reviews are from truck owners.
