Who we are

Built on paint work — and word of mouth.

Khan Auto Detailing Service brings professional paint care to Houston drivers wherever their vehicle is parked. The work centres on the services that make the biggest visible difference: ceramic coating, paint correction, buffing & polishing and headlight restoration.

The business is run by its owner, Khan, who handles the work himself. That's why reviews mention him by name — and why customers keep coming back with their second car or truck.

Its reputation has been earned one driveway at a time: a 4.9-star average across 80 Google reviews, with reviews going back more than four years.
