01 / Protection

Ceramic Coating

The service Khan is best known for. A ceramic coating bonds to your clear coat to create a hard, high-gloss, water-repelling layer that guards against UV rays, road grime and contaminants — and keeps the car easier to wash for years, not weeks.

Price: Request a quote
Good to know
  • Multi-year coating options available
  • Deep gloss with strong water beading
  • Protection from UV, contaminants and light scratches
  • Often paired with paint correction first

02 / Restoration

Paint Correction

Machine correction that removes swirl marks, scratches and oxidation to restore your vehicle's original depth and colour. It's the step that makes faded or swirled paint look new again — and the best base for a ceramic coating.

Price: Request a quote
Targets
  • Swirl marks and holograms
  • Light scratches and scuffs
  • Oxidised, dull or faded paint

Buffing & Polishing

Buffing removes surface imperfections; polishing refines the paint to a deep, smooth, mirror-like shine. Ideal when your paint just needs its gloss brought back — for cars, trucks and SUVs alike.

Good for

  • Full-size trucks and SUVs
