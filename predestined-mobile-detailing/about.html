Your vehicle, treated like it’s ours.

Predestined Mobile Detailing, LLC is a Houston mobile detailing company run by its owner, Terrence. The promise is right there in our motto — and 193 Google reviews say we keep it.

Who we are

Owner-operated, start to finish.

When you book Predestined, you’re dealing with Terrence — the person who answers your message, gives you the quote and does the work. Customers describe him as professional, responsive and easy to talk to (one review adds: He has a sense of humor too!).

The company runs from branded mobile vans, so everything needed for the job comes to you: driveways, office lots, garages. The work ranges from family SUVs full of dog hair to Lamborghinis, Bentleys, RVs — and, as the photo shows, even an aircraft.
