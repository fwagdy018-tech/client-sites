FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Call or text (832) 498-5934.

Do you come to me?

Yes — Predestined is a fully mobile detailing service. Terrence comes to your home, office or wherever your vehicle is parked.

Which areas do you serve?

Houston and the surrounding area. If you're not sure whether you're in range, call or text (832) 498-5934 and we'll let you know.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday 8 AM – 5 PM and Sunday 8 AM – 3 PM, as listed on Google.

How much does a detail cost?

Every quote is based on your vehicle's size, its condition and the service you choose. Send a quote request or call with your vehicle details and you'll get a price for your car, truck or SUV. Reviewers often mention the price being affordable for the work done.

Can you get out pet hair, spills and odors?

Yes. Dog hair removal, spilled-coffee cleanups and deep interior cleaning come up again and again in our Google reviews — interior deep cleans are one of our core services.

Do you work on trucks, SUVs, RVs and exotic cars?

Yes. Recent jobs include full-size trucks and SUVs, an RV, a Lamborghini, a Bentley, a Mercedes G-Class and a Rolls-Royce engine bay — see the gallery.

How soon can I get an appointment?

It depends on the schedule that week, but customers have been seen the next day — and in one case within two hours of messaging. Call or text to ask about the earliest slot.

How do I book?

Call or text (832) 498-5934, message us on Facebook, or send the quote form. Appointments are confirmed directly with you — nothing is booked until you hear back.
