FAQ

Good to know

Do you come to me?

Yes. Grant Mobile Detail is a mobile service. Google reviewers say they were detailed at home, at an apartment, at work, in a gas station lot and even at a hotel. Call or text to confirm your location.

Which areas do you serve?

Fort Worth and Arlington, TX, and customers across the DFW Metroplex mention the service in their reviews. No official service-area list is published, so call or text with your neighborhood or ZIP and you will get a straight answer.

How much does it cost?

Prices depend on the vehicle and the work it needs, so there is no fixed price list. Request a free quote or call. Reviewers repeatedly call the pricing “very reasonable.”

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the vehicle. One reviewer was quoted 2-3 hours and the work ran past 4 hours at no extra charge. Ask for an estimate when you book.

How do I book?

Google lists the business as appointment required. Call or text +1 817-716-8266, email Thedetailguy817@gmail.com, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message. Nothing is booked until you get confirmation.

What do you need at my location?

Reviewers describe a self-contained setup: a van with its own water and electric, a pressure washer, a generator and a pop-up tent over the car. One customer had a detail done at their apartment. Call or text before the appointment to ask if anything is needed.

What vehicles do you detail?

Cars, trucks, SUVs and vans are all in the reviews, and the Google photos also show sports cars, classics and boats. If it is something unusual, just ask.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM, and closed on Sunday. Call or text to schedule, since appointments depend on the day.
