Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

The shop’s window sign says walk-ins are always welcome. You can also book online through Booksy, or call (210) 966-7834.

What are your hours?

As listed on Google: Monday to Thursday 9 AM – 7 PM, Friday and Saturday 9 AM – 9 PM, Sunday 9 AM – 5 PM. Hours can change, so call to confirm before you come.

How much is a haircut?

There is no official price list online. A window sign in a Google photo of the shop reads “Cuts starting at $35”, but prices can change. Call (210) 966-7834 to confirm.

Where are you located?

11817 West Ave, San Antonio, TX 78216, in Parliament Square II.

Do you do kids’ cuts, color, perms or facials?

Kids haircuts, hair coloring, curly perms, facials and hair straightener are all listed on the shop’s windows and door. Call to confirm what is available and how long it takes.
