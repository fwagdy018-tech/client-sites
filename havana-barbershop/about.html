The shop

Walk in, sit down, say what you want.

You will find Havana Barbershop at 11817 West Ave, inside Parliament Square II. The shop is open seven days a week, and the sign on the window says walk-ins are always welcome.

On Google the business is listed as Latino-owned and LGBTQ+ friendly, and Google reviewers mention that Spanish is spoken.
