Google lists the salon as appointments recommended, appointment required and accepts walk-ins. The safest move is to call or text 361-452-1013 and ask what is open.

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Sunday and Monday (as shown on Google Maps). Please confirm before a holiday.
