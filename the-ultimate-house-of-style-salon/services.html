How pricing works

Ask first, no surprises.

The salon does not publish a price list online, and we would rather not guess. Call or text 361-452-1013, describe what you want, and ask for a quote before you book.

Helpful to include: a photo of what you want, a photo of your hair now, and your current length. Payments listed on Google: credit and debit cards, NFC mobile payments and cheques.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Do I need an appointment?

Google lists the salon as appointments recommended, appointment required and accepts walk-ins. The safest move is to call or text 361-452-1013 and ask what is open.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Sunday and Monday (as shown on Google Maps). Please confirm before a holiday.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published online. Call or text 361-452-1013 for a quote for the service you want.

Do you take kids?

The Google listing marks the salon as good for kids, and one reviewer describes a stylist who was patient with her son. Ask when you call.

How can I pay?

Google lists credit cards, debit cards, NFC mobile payments and cheques.

Do you rent salon rooms to stylists?
