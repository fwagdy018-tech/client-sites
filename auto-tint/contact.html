FAQ

Good to know

Where is the shop?

11293 Fuqua St, Houston, TX 77089 (South Belt / Ellington area). Use the directions button on the contact page.

What services do you offer?

Car window tinting for cars, SUVs, trucks and vans, ceramic window tint, tint replacement, and commercial and residential window tinting, per the shop's sign and Google reviews. Call to ask about anything not listed.

How much does it cost?

No price list is published. Reviewers repeatedly mention good prices, and the shop's sign says free estimate. Call or send the quote form and get a number for your vehicle before work starts.

How long does it take?

Reviewers describe jobs done the same day, some in about an hour and a single window in about 30 minutes. Timing depends on the vehicle and the film, so ask when you call.

Do you offer a warranty?

Owner replies on Google mention a one-year warranty, and one reviewer mentions a 5-year warranty. Coverage can vary by film, so ask for the exact terms before you pay.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Saturday 9 AM to 7 PM and Sunday 11 AM to 6 PM. Call ahead to confirm, especially on holidays.

Do I need an appointment?

Not listed as required. One reviewer stopped in for a quote and had the job done that day, but calling ahead is the surest way to get a time.
