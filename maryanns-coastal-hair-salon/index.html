Questions

Before you call.

Do I need an appointment?

Reviews mention regular visits and a few last-minute fits. Call 361-353-4854 first and the salon can tell you what is open.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Friday 10 AM – 5 PM, Saturday 9:30 AM – 1 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday, as shown on Google. Please confirm before a holiday.

How much does it cost?

Prices are not published online, and your price depends on length and service. Call or send a request and ask for a quote before you book.

Can I book wedding or event hair?

Several reviewers had updos or hair done for weddings. Call early with your date and bring an inspiration photo.

Where is the salon?

902 Waldron Rd, Corpus Christi, TX 78418, in the Flour Bluff area. Directions are one tap away on this site.

Do you do teen or kids’ hair?

Reviewers mention a teenage son and a daughter getting braids for a dance competition. Ask when you call about age and the style you want.
