Good to know

Do you come to me?

Yes. MBK Details describes itself on Facebook and Instagram as shop and fully mobile, and many Google reviewers say the team came to their home or work. Call or text to confirm they can reach your location.

Which areas do you serve?

Hidalgo County and more, based in Edinburg, TX in the Rio Grande Valley. If you're farther out, just call or text and ask.

How much does it cost?

Prices depend on the vehicle, its condition and the service, so no price list is posted. Call or text (956) 200-8620 for a free quote, and several Google reviewers describe the pricing as fair.

How do I book?

Call or text +1 956-200-8620, email mbkdetails@gmail.com, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message to the shop. Nothing is booked until MBK Details confirms with you.

Can you get stains out of my seats?

Stain removal and seat and carpet shampoo or extraction are part of the shop's services, and reviewers mention food, drink and water stains that came out. Results depend on the stain, so send a photo when you ask for a quote.

Do you offer ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating is listed on the shop's Facebook and Instagram. Ask for a quote and which protection fits your vehicle and budget.
