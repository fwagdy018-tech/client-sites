Who we are

Detailing that comes to you

MBK Details is a car detailing business based in Edinburg, Texas, in the Rio Grande Valley. The shop describes itself as shop and fully mobile, serving Hidalgo County and more. Instead of driving to a shop, you can have the work come to your driveway, your office or wherever the vehicle is parked.

The shop’s own tagline is “Where precision meets shine,” and its services are interior, exterior and ceramic coating, plus carpet and seat shampoo, extraction, wax, pet hair removal and vehicle restoration. On Google, customers keep calling the team punctual, professional and great at communicating.
