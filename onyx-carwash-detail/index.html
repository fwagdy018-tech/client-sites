Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Call or text (956) 595-9261 and ask.

Do you come to me?

Yes. ONYX Carwash & Detail is a mobile service — Jose Luis comes to your home or work, so you never have to leave the comfort of your house. Customers also mention that he can pick up and return their vehicle; ask about it when you book.

Which areas do you serve?

McAllen and Pharr, and the surrounding Rio Grande Valley. If you’re in Edinburg, Mission, San Juan or nearby, call or text (956) 595-9261 to confirm we cover your address.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted for your vehicle — the price depends on its size, its condition and the service you choose. Send the year, make and model and what you’d like done, and you’ll get a free quote. Reviewers regularly describe the prices as affordable and “worth more than listed.”

What are your hours?

Google lists ONYX as open 24 hours. In practice, appointments are set by call or text — tell us what time works for you and we’ll confirm it.

Do you detail trucks?

All the time. “Truck wash” is the most-mentioned service in our Google reviews — from Raptors and TRXs to work trucks and daily drivers.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Puede llamar o mandar mensaje en español al (956) 595-9261. Calidad y un buen precio como usted se merece.

What do you need from me?

Just the vehicle and a spot to work, like your driveway. Let us know about anything special — pets, stains, a car seat — when you book.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle and the service. A wash is quick; a full inside-and-out detail takes longer. You’ll get a time estimate with your quote.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request by text message. Jose Luis replies to confirm the price and the time.
