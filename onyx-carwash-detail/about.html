Our story

A carwash that shows up at your door.

ONYX Carwash & Detail is a mobile car wash and detailing service run by Jose Luis in McAllen and the Rio Grande Valley. His promise is right on the logo: “Calidad y un buen precio como tú mereces” — quality, and a good price, like you deserve.

Instead of a drive-through tunnel, you get one person who takes his time with your car. Customers describe him as respectful, friendly and punctual, and several have been with him for years — some say they wouldn’t take their car anywhere else.

From daily-driver sedans and family SUVs to lifted trucks, Corvettes and a Porsche 911, every vehicle gets the same care: spotless inside and out, with the small details done right.
