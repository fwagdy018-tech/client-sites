About us

Veteran-owned.Detail-driven.

Brazos Valley Mobile Detailing is a Texas veteran-owned and operated business based in Grimes County, serving the Brazos Valley and Greater Houston since 2024.

The owner’s own logo: “Get Your Shine On · Est. 2024”

The story

Professional service at competitive pricing.

That’s the owner’s mission in his own words, and customers keep repeating it. Frank, a Texas veteran, started Brazos Valley Mobile Detailing in 2024 and brings the work to you, whether that’s a car in a driveway, a motor coach, a tractor or a limestone house.

He details cars, trucks and fleets, boats, heavy construction and agricultural equipment, and he soft washes siding, delicate paint and concrete. Reviewers call him honest, hardworking, responsive and kind, and 89 of 89 Google reviews are five stars.
