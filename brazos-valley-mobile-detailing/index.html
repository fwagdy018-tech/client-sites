FAQ

Good to know before you book.

Still have a question? Call or text (936) 870-5337. We’re open every day.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Brazos Valley Mobile Detailing is fully mobile. We detail at your home, your workplace or your job site, whether that’s a driveway, a barn or a ranch.

What area do you serve?

We’re based in Grimes County and travel across the Brazos Valley and Greater Houston area, including College Station, Bryan, Navasota, Magnolia, Montgomery, Cypress, Tomball, Waller and Hempstead. Outside those towns? Call or text your location and we’ll tell you if we can get to you.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is customized to the project’s condition and location, and quotes are free with no obligation. Text or call (936) 870-5337 with what you need cleaned (photos help) and you’ll have a price before we start. Customers often mention the prices in their reviews.

Do you offer fleet pricing?

Yes. Fleet pricing is available on request for work trucks, company vehicles and equipment. Call or text to talk it through.

What can you clean besides cars?

Trucks, SUVs, RVs, horse trailers, boats, tractors and construction equipment, plus driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, siding, limestone and windows. If you’re not sure we do it, ask.

Can you remove scratches?

Often, yes. Customers describe scratches polished out completely. Deeper scratches can’t always be fully removed, so we look at the paint with you first and tell you honestly what to expect.

What are your hours?

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday 8 AM – 6:30 PM, Thursday 8 AM – 6 PM, Saturday and Sunday 10 AM – 6:30 PM, as listed on Google.

Are you veteran-owned and insured?

Yes. The owner is a Texas veteran, and the business is described on its own site as a state-designated Texas Veteran-Owned Small Business registered on SAM.gov, carrying a $1,000,000 commercial general liability policy.

Do I have to be there?

Not necessarily. Tell us where the vehicle or property is and what works for you when you book, and we’ll confirm the details with you directly.
