FAQ

Good to know

Where is Detail Matters based?

Detail Matters is a family-owned auto detailing business in Rockport, TX. Mobile detailing is available, so call or text to confirm that we can reach your location.

What are your hours?

Google lists 7 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week. An appointment is required, so call or text first.

How much does it cost?

Interior and Exterior packages are $75 (small), $100 (mid-size) or $125 (full-size). The Deluxe package, which includes both, is $150, $200 or $250. Add-ons start at $30. Additional fees may apply for pet hair.

Do you come to me?

Yes, mobile detailing is available. The mobile service fee starts at $50 and varies with travel distance.

Is there a first-time customer deal?

Yes. First-time customers save 20% on Deluxe packages, per the current Detail Matters menu.

How do I book?

Call or text +1 361-205-4083, email detailmatters23@gmail.com, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message to the shop. Nothing is booked until Detail Matters confirms with you. Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments are listed on Google.
