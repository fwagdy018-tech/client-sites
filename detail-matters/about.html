Who we are

Family-owned, detail-obsessed

Detail Matters Co. LLC is a family-owned auto detailing business in Rockport, TX, paying attention since 2023. In the shop's own words: “We’re dedicated to providing exceptional care, building trust, and making every detail count.”

Google reviewers describe the owner, Dustin, as professional, kind, humble and quick to respond, with fair, clearly quoted prices. The shop is open 7 AM to 7 PM, seven days a week, by appointment, and mobile detailing is available.
