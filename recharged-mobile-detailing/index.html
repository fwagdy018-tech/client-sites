FAQ

Good to know before you book.

Still have a question? Call or text (806) 626-3835. We’re open every day.

Do you really come to me?

Yes, that’s the whole idea. We detail at your home or at your work. Customers in our reviews mention both, and we’ve also detailed RVs where they were parked.

Do I have to be there while you work?

Not necessarily. Plenty of customers hand over the keys at work and get them back when the job is done. Just let us know what works for you when you book.

What area do you serve?

We’re a mobile detailer based in Amarillo, Texas. If you’re outside town, call or text your location and we’ll tell you if we can get to you.

How much does a detail cost?

Every vehicle is quoted on its own size, its condition and the services you want. Text or call (806) 626-3835 with the year, make and model (photos help) and we’ll give you a price before we start. Customers often call our pricing fair and reasonable.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM – 6 PM, Saturday 10 AM – 6 PM and Sunday 10 AM – 5 PM, as listed on Google.

Can you get dog hair out?

Yes. Pet hair removal is one of the things our customers mention most, including dogs, labs and the whole family car.

Do you detail trucks, SUVs and RVs?

Yes. Pickups, SUVs and motor coaches are a big part of what we do. Truck detailing is the most mentioned topic in our Google reviews.

Can you remove scratches?

Light scratches, paint splatter and dull paint can often be polished out by machine. Deeper scratches can’t always be fully removed, so we look at the paint with you first and tell you honestly what to expect.

Are you veteran-owned?

Yes. Recharged Mobile Detailing LLC is listed on Google as veteran-owned.
