Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Call or text (956) 622-1018 and ask.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Simple Shine Mobile Detailing comes to your home, so you never have to leave the driveway — customers call it “time liberating.” Call or text (956) 622-1018 to book.

Which areas do you serve?

The Rio Grande Valley, Texas. A customer describes Mark as serving “the Mid-Valley and Valley-wide area.” Call or text (956) 622-1018 with your town to confirm he can reach you.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted for your vehicle, because price depends on its size, its condition and the service you choose. Reviewers describe the pricing as “fair,” “reasonable and affordable,” and “similar to taking it to the car wash and leaving a tip.” Send the year, make and model and you’ll get a free quote.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Saturday and Sunday, and appointments are required. Call or text to find a time — customers say Mark is flexible with busy schedules.

Do you work on trucks, motorcycles and RVs?

Yes. Reviews mention pickups, SUVs, a Harley-Davidson, a class B motorhome, a golf cart and a matte-paint car, alongside everyday sedans.

Can you clean up kid and pet messes?

Customers say so: one mom of two praised how clean the carpets and cup holders looked afterwards, and another had a landscaping-ruined SUV interior fixed. Tell Mark what you’re dealing with when you ask for a quote.

What do you need from me?

Just the vehicle and a spot to work, like your driveway. Let Mark know about anything special — matte paint, pets, stains, a car seat — when you book.

Can you do several vehicles in one visit?

Yes. Several reviewers had two or three vehicles detailed in one visit. Mention how many when you ask for a quote.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request by text message. Mark replies to confirm the price and the time, and he’ll check the vehicle with you before he leaves so he can touch up anything.
