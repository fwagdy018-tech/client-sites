Our story

A detailer who shows up at your home.

Simple Shine Mobile Detailing brings car detailing to you. Instead of a drive-through tunnel or a drop-off, you get one person who takes his time — customers say he “didn’t rush at all,” “takes his time for perfection,” and “pays attention to all the little details.”

Reviewers on Google describe a first visit that turned into a monthly routine, a busy traveler who can always get on his calendar, a family that had three vehicles done in one visit and a customer who calls Mark “my exclusive detailer.” Another wrote that he is “committed to serving the Mid-Valley and Valley-wide area.”

From everyday sedans and family SUVs to crew-cab trucks, a matte-paint car, Harley-Davidsons, a golf cart and a class B motorhome, every vehicle gets the same care.
