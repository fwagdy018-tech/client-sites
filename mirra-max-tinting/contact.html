FAQ

Good to know

Where is Mirra Max Tinting?

The shop is at 11394 James Watt Dr #305, El Paso, TX 79936, as listed on Google. Tap Directions on the contact page to open it in Maps.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM, and closed on Sunday. Call to confirm holiday hours or a specific time.

What vehicles do you tint?

Reviewers mention a Corvette, a Ford F-150, a Tesla, a Toyota Corolla and a fully tinted Expedition Max, and the gallery shows trucks, SUVs, sedans and sports cars. Call if you have something different: the gallery also shows film on storefront glass and a tractor cab.

How much does it cost?

No price list is published. Price depends on the vehicle and the film you choose, for example ceramic. Reviewers call the pricing fair and very good, and one says the owner priced out several films for them. Call, text or use the quote form for a straight number.

Is there a warranty?

Google highlights lifetime warranty in 20 reviews, and one reviewer says they were told 10 years on the film and lifetime on the workmanship. Ask for the exact terms when you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Reviewers describe booking a specific date and time and dropping the car off in the morning, so call or text first. Ask for an estimated finish time when you drop off.

How do I book?

Call or text +1 915-694-5057, or use the quote form on this site. The form prepares a text message to the shop. Nothing is booked until they confirm the day and time with you.
