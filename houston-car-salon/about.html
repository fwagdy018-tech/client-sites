About us

A neighborhood shopwith a salon finish.

Houston Car Salon is a local, hands-on car wash and detail shop on N Shepherd Dr in the Heights — a small business that treats every car like it’s going somewhere special.

Who we are

Hand work, done the careful way.

We do it all by hand — from a quick Basic Wash on your lunch break to a Deluxe Detail with shampooed carpets, conditioned leather and a clay bar treatment for the paint. Our menu is posted on the wall with clear prices for every vehicle size, from compacts to dualies and wreckers.

We’re a small, owner-run team, and our regulars know us by name. Customers mention the owner being on site and “always super friendly,” and a crew that goes “above and beyond.”
