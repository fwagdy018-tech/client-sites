FAQ

Good to know before you pull in.

Still have a question? Call (713) 505-7477 — we’re open every day.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins and drive-ups are welcome seven days a week — just pull in and we’ll help you right away. If you’re coming for a big detail, calling ahead is always a good idea.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday 7:45 AM – 7 PM and Sunday 8:45 AM – 6 PM, as listed on Google. One regular’s tip: around 8:30–9 AM is usually less busy.

How much does a wash cost?

A Basic Wash starts at $25 for a car, $32 for an SUV, $37 for vans, trucks and Suburbans and $43 for dualies and wreckers. Full Detailing starts at $145. Every price is on the Services & Prices page. Prices may vary with the size and condition of the vehicle.

How long does it take?

Standard washes are quick — customers describe being in and out in 15 to 30 minutes. Full and deluxe details take longer; we’ll give you a time when you drop off.

Can you get dog hair out?

Yes. Dog hair vacuum cleaning is a $20 add-on, and pet hair removal is one of the things customers praise most in our reviews.

Do you work on trucks and big vehicles?

Yes — our menu is priced for cars, SUVs, vans, trucks, Suburbans, dualies and wreckers.

Do you remove scratches?

We offer a High Speed Buff for scratches in the paint: $280 for a small car, $320 for an SUV and $350 for vans, trucks and Suburbans. Bring the vehicle by so we can look at the paint first.

Is the shop accessible?

Yes. Google lists a wheelchair-accessible car park, entrance and restroom.
