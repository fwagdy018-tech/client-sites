A shop people come back to

We only say what the shop’s own Google listing and its customers say. Here is what stands out.

The shop

West Waco, one chair at a time

Look At Me Now Barbershop is a barber shop at 2401 W Waco Dr d in Waco, Texas. Its Google listing shows black barber chairs, a wood floor, ring lights over the stations and a row of waiting chairs by the front windows.

Customers describe a friendly, clean shop where whichever chair you land in, you get a great cut. Several mention bringing their kids, and one says they drive in from Temple just for a trim.

The shop takes walk-ins, and you can also call, text or WhatsApp ahead.
