Good to know

Quick answers

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

Both. A sign in the shop window says walk-ins welcome, and a reviewer says someone is always ready to cut. You can also call, text or WhatsApp (254) 235-7172 to ask for a time. Another reviewer suggests booking ahead, but says it is not required.

What are your hours?

As shown on Google: Tuesday to Friday 9 AM – 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM – 6 PM, closed Sunday and Monday. Hours can change, so please call to confirm before you come.

How much is a haircut?

Prices are not listed on this site because we do not have an official price list to show. Call, text or WhatsApp (254) 235-7172 to confirm current prices.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Google reviewers list kid-friendly and toddler haircuts, and parents describe bringing their sons, nephews and little ones in.

How do I ask for a time?

Call (254) 235-7172, or use the Text and WhatsApp buttons to message the same number. This site has no online booking, so the shop confirms every time itself.
