A barber shop on Babcock.

Petra Barbershop is a full-service barber shop in Northgate Plaza, San Antonio, offering haircuts, fades, beard trims and shaves.

The shop

Walk in, sit down, say what you want.

You will find Petra Barbershop at 5500 Babcock Rd #114, on the first floor of Northgate Plaza. The shop is open Tuesday to Sunday and closed on Mondays.

Google lists walk-ins as accepted and appointments as recommended. The shop is also listed as good for kids, with a wheelchair-accessible entrance, car park and restroom.
