The shop

A bright, clean room on Babcock Road.

Petra Barbershop is a full-service barber shop in Northgate Plaza. Come in for a haircut, a fade, a beard trim or a shave. Walk-ins are accepted and appointments are recommended.

Customers describe the shop as clean and friendly and the barbers as detail-oriented. Their own words are further down this page.

Questions, answered.

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

Google lists Petra Barbershop as accepting walk-ins, with appointments recommended. To avoid a wait, call (210) 561-5550 before you come.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 10:30 AM – 7 PM, Sunday 10:30 AM – 6 PM. Closed on Mondays. Hours can change for holidays, so give the shop a call to confirm.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices are not published online. Call (210) 561-5550 and the shop can tell you what to expect for the service you want.

Where are you located?

5500 Babcock Rd #114, San Antonio, TX 78240, in Northgate Plaza on the first floor.
