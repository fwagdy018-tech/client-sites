FAQ

Good to know

Where is the shop?

5802 Wooldridge Rd #E1, Corpus Christi, TX 78414 (South Side). Use the directions button on the contact page.

What services do you offer?

Auto window tint including ceramic, windshield, sunroof and eyebrow strips, taillight blackouts and emblem wraps, home and commercial window tint, privacy film, and film removal and replacement, based on the shop's banner and Google reviews. Call to ask about anything not listed.

How much does it cost?

No price list is published. Reviewers say the shop is up front about tint options and price, and many call the prices fair. Call or send the quote form and get a number for your vehicle before work starts.

How long does it take?

Reviewers describe jobs finished in under two hours, and two windows done in about 30 minutes. Timing depends on the vehicle and film, so ask when you call.

Do you offer a warranty?

One reviewer mentions a lifetime warranty and others say the shop stands behind its work. Coverage can vary by film, so ask for the exact terms before you pay.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Friday 9 AM to 7 PM, closed Saturday and Sunday. Call ahead to confirm, especially around holidays.

Do I need an appointment?

Reviewers both scheduled ahead and walked in to look at tint options, so either seems to work. Calling ahead is the surest way to get a time.
