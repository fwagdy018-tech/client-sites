Est. 1967

An old-fashioned barbershop

Castle Hills Barber Shop is at 2313 NW Military Highway in San Antonio. Its name on Google reads Est. 1967, and the shop leans into that spirit: brick walls, red-and-chrome barber chairs, barber poles on the windows.

The shop’s owner describes the goal in a reply to a Google review: an affordable price for the family, and a return to the old-fashioned barbershop atmosphere.

…this is what we want to be for this community and have a affordable price for the family to rescue the old fashioned barbershop atmosphere and culture.From the shop owner’s reply to a Google review, 4 years ago

What customers keep mentioning

Topics from the shop’s Google reviews. The numbers show how many reviews mention each one, as listed on Google November 2026.

Friendly, family feel

Reviewers describe being treated like family, and staff who are kind to kids. Google lists the shop as good for kids.

Easygoing and fair

Reviewers mention a reasonable price, quick turnaround and refreshments while you wait. One notes the staff are bilingual in English and Spanish.

Atmosphere with character

A 1950s jukebox, vintage-style chairs, a doll corner for little ones. Reviewers notice the details.
