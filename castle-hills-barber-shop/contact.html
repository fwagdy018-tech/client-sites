Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

The Google listing says the shop accepts walk-ins and that appointments are recommended. To save yourself a wait, call (210) 944-2795.

What are your hours?

As shown on Google: Monday 12 PM – 5 PM, Tuesday to Friday 9 AM – 6 PM, Saturday 9 AM – 5 PM, closed Sunday. Hours can change, so please call to confirm before you come.

How much is a haircut?

There is no official price list online, so we do not quote one. One Google reviewer wrote “$30 for a fantastic cut”, but prices can change. Call (210) 944-2795 to confirm.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Google lists the shop as good for kids, and reviewers describe first haircuts and school-year haircuts for their children.

How can I pay?

Google lists credit cards, debit cards, NFC mobile payments and cheques. Call if you have a question about payment.

Where are you and where do I park?

2313 NW Military Hwy #107, San Antonio, TX 78231. A sign in the shop window reads “Barber Shop Parking Only”. Google lists a wheelchair-accessible entrance and car park.
