FAQ

Good to know

Where are you located?

816 Berry Rd, Houston, TX 77022, in the Northside / Northline area. Use the directions button to open it in your maps app.

What are your hours?

Google lists Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM, and closed on Sunday. Reviewers suggest arriving early, since some were second in line at opening.

How much does window tint cost?

No price list is published, and the price depends on your vehicle and the film. One reviewer says they were quoted $300 including removal for all windows and paid $260, and several call the pricing the best they found. Call for an estimate.

How long does it take?

Reviewers report times from about 20 minutes to under an hour once the work starts. Call ahead to ask about the current wait.

Do you do ceramic tint?

Reviewers mention ceramic tint by name. Ask the front desk which films are available for your vehicle and how they compare.

Do I need an appointment?

Google does not list the shop as appointment-only, and reviewers mention arriving early and waiting their turn. You can call ahead for an estimate, and a reviewer says the staff are helpful with questions.

Do you do car audio and alarms?

Car audio is in the business name, the bay shows BOSS Audio signage and the shop banner lists car alarms. The details are not published, so call to ask what is available for your vehicle.
