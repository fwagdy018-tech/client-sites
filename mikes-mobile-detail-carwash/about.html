Our story

A carwash that shows up at your door.

Mike’s Mobile Detail Carwash is a mobile car wash and detailing service in El Paso, Texas. Instead of a drive-through tunnel, you get one person who takes his time with your vehicle — and who, as customers put it, “has a system that shows a process from beginning to the end result.”

Customers have been reviewing Mike on Google for more than six years. Some have used him since their children were born, one business owner has sent him 20–25 vehicles for their customers, and others book him every month. Reviewers also mention his team: “Mike and his co-worker.”

From daily-driver sedans and family SUVs to lifted trucks, a Tesla, a Hummer EV, Harleys and a 32-foot RV, every vehicle gets the same care.
