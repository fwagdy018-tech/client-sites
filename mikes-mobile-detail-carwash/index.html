Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Call or text (915) 588-3559 and ask.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Mike’s Mobile Detail Carwash comes to your home or work, so you never have to leave the house — customers say it’s “super convenient.” Mike’s phone is (915) 588-3559; call or text to book.

Which areas do you serve?

El Paso, Texas. If you’re in Horizon City, Socorro, Canutillo or nearby, call or text (915) 588-3559 to confirm Mike can reach your address.

How much does it cost?

Every job is quoted for your vehicle — the price depends on its size, its condition and the service you choose. Send the year, make and model and what you’d like done, and you’ll get a free quote. Reviewers describe the rates as “reasonable” and “great.”

What are your hours?

Google and local directories list Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM, closed Sunday. Appointments are set by call or text — a few reviewers mention early-morning appointments, so ask what time works.

Do you work on trucks, RVs and motorcycles?

Yes. Reviews mention a 32 ft. RV, Harley-Davidson motorcycles, lifted trucks, a ’65 Mustang and a matte-finish Hummer EV, alongside everyday cars and SUVs.

Can you remove stains and odors?

Customers say so: reviewers describe pet accidents, coffee stains, drink spills and a carpet odor taken care of with steam cleaning. Every vehicle is different, so tell Mike what you’re dealing with when you ask for a quote.

What do you need from me?

Just the vehicle and a spot to work, like your driveway. Let Mike know about anything special — pets, stains, matte paint, a car seat — when you book.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle and the service. One reviewer booked an interior steam clean and engine wash for 8 AM and was done by 10 AM. You’ll get a time estimate with your quote.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request by text message. Mike replies to confirm the price and the time, and customers say he sends a confirmation the night before.
